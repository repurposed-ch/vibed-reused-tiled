Vibed · Reused · Tiled

Industrial materials are calibrated to demand. Reused ones are not. Reuse starts from a limited stock of tiles with rough dimensions, rough properties and only a rough idea of how many of each kind there will be.

So instead of one fixed solution, the designer describes an intention, a vibe: a pattern, a texture, a material quality, a cultural reference. The tools here explore how to describe that vibe while keeping control over both design and construction.

How the app works

Six steps, each one a page in the app. Jump straight into any of them.

  1. 01 Tiles & materials Define tile types, sizes and material presets.
  2. 02 Stock Roughly how many of each tile to expect.
  3. 03 Design family Describe the vibe as a schema, not one fixed layout.
  4. 04 Boundaries The real region that has to be tiled.
  5. 05 Solve Fit the family to the stock inside the boundary.
  6. 06 2D & 3D views Review and export the result.
